Delete Your Lure Therapy Tournaments Account

Use this page to request deletion of your Lure Therapy Tournaments account and associated personal information.

How to request deletion

Email us from the email address associated with your Lure Therapy Tournaments account.

To: mat_p86@hotmail.com
Subject: Lure Therapy Tournaments – Account Deletion Request

Email Deletion Request

What will be deleted

Where reasonably possible, deletion may include your profile and account information, account-linked tournament registrations, saved personal information, push notification tokens and other personal information associated with your account.

What may be retained

Some information may need to be retained or anonymised where reasonably necessary for legitimate tournament records, competition integrity, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, safety requirements or legal obligations. Historical competition results may remain in an anonymised or limited form where necessary to preserve tournament records.

Before you request deletion

Account deletion may permanently remove your access to your profile, tournament registrations, catch history and other information associated with your account. Deletion may not be reversible.

Questions

If you have questions about deletion or privacy, email mat_p86@hotmail.com.
