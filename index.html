WHY WE BUILT IT

More time on the water.
Less time on paperwork.

We know how much work goes into running a fishing competition. Lure Therapy Tournaments was developed to help fishing clubs handle the organising, scoring and communication without juggling paper forms or multiple systems.

Event hosting is by request: Fishing clubs contact us to request an event. We create and set it up, then nominated tournament directors and catch approvers can use their assigned tools.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

The tools your club needs to run a CPR tournament.

From registrations to final results, keep the important parts of your competition together.

01

Tournament management

Lure Therapy creates and configures your event after a hosting request, with organiser tools for nominated club officials.

02

Catch, photo & release

Anglers photograph fish on a measuring mat and submit species, lengths and catch details digitally.

03

Catch verification

Dedicated approvers can review photos, approve catches or reject entries with a reason.

04

Live leaderboards

Competitors can follow tournament standings as approved results are added.

05

Individual & team events

Support solo anglers and teams with competition formats that suit your club.

06

Competition integrity

Camera-only submissions, location tools and digital key tags help organisers verify entries.

07

Event control & updates

Tournament directors can manage on-water status and communicate important event updates.

08

Offline catch submissions

With event details loaded beforehand, anglers can save catches without reception for upload when service returns.

Tournament rules, eligibility, safety decisions and the use of verification tools remain subject to each event's settings. Live updates require connectivity.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple process from start to finish.

Built to support organisers without making things complicated for anglers.

01 / PLAN

Request your event

Send us the dates, format and species. We create and configure the competition for your club.

02 / JOIN

Anglers get involved

Competitors join the event in the app and review the tournament details.

03 / SUBMIT

Catch, photo, release

Anglers measure, photograph and submit catches, even offline when prepared in advance.

04 / VERIFY

Review the results

Approvers check catches, and the leaderboard updates as entries are verified.
