Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

Open Lure Therapy Tournaments and select Create Account to register, or Login if you already have an account.

I've forgotten my password. What should I do?

From the login screen, tap Forgot Password? and follow the instructions sent to your account email. Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the message.

How do I join a fishing tournament?

Open the app's Events area to view available tournaments. Select an event to review its information and joining instructions. Some events may require a tournament code or have eligibility restrictions.

How do I submit a catch?

Choose the relevant tournament, open Submit Catch, select the fish species, enter its measured length and take the required catch photo. Follow the event's measuring-mat and photo rules before submitting your entry for review.

Why isn't my catch on the leaderboard?

Some submissions require verification by tournament administrators before they appear in official results. Check your submission status and the event rules. If something looks wrong, email support with the event name and details of the entry.

Where can I find live results?

Open Live Events to find active competitions and their scoreboards. You can also review the details of tournaments you have joined.

Camera or location access isn't working. What can I do?

Check the app permissions in your iPhone or Android device settings. Allow the camera and location access needed for the relevant feature, then reopen the app. Some tournaments may require location details to verify a catch.

How do I delete my account?

See our Account Deletion page for instructions on requesting deletion of your account and associated personal data.
